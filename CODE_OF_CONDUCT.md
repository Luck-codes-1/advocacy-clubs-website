# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place for people with
different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and critique contributions without attacking people.
- Welcome constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information
  without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing
private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub.
Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and
handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.
